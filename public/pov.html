Share a mirrored phone from a computer

Open your private link on the capture computer, then share the mirror window.

  • iPhone with Windows: connect a compatible iPhone video adapter to an HDMI capture card. In OBS, add the card as a Video Capture Device and open its windowed projector.
  • iPhone with Mac: connect by USB, open QuickTime Player, choose New Movie Recording, and select the iPhone as the source. Leave the preview open.
  • Android: open the player-approved scrcpy mirror with scrcpy --no-control --no-audio.
