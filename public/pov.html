If PRISM says “The RTMP connection … has failed” (1_1 / 150), tap OK, wait 30 seconds, then tap Go Live again. Your codes stay the same. If it keeps failing, tell the staff.

Locking your iPhone stops the stream. Do this once before your match.
  1. Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never. Turn Low Power Mode off, because it locks the phone after 30 seconds.
  2. To stop the side button from locking the phone by accident, go to Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access, turn it on and set a passcode. In your game, press the side button 3 times, tap Options, turn Side Button off, then tap Start. To leave, press it 3 times again and type the passcode.
  3. If the stream stops anyway, keep playing first. At your next safe moment, open PRISM and tap Go Live. Your codes are saved, so this takes about 10 seconds. Tell your facilitator it dropped, so a gap in your stream is not counted against you.
Share a mirrored phone from a computer

Open your private link on the capture computer, then share the mirror window.

  • iPhone with Windows: connect a compatible iPhone video adapter to an HDMI capture card. In OBS, add the card as a Video Capture Device and open its windowed projector.
  • iPhone with Mac: connect by USB, open QuickTime Player, choose New Movie Recording, and select the iPhone as the source. Leave the preview open.
  • Android: open the player-approved scrcpy mirror with scrcpy --no-control --no-audio.
