ARENAHUB MATCH MONITOR

Record your match on iPhone

iPhones cannot run the ArenaHub Companion. Instead, you record your own screen and upload the video here after the match.

1. Pair with the referee

What you agree to:

  • You upload a screen recording of this match. It goes to the referee laptop at the venue, not to the internet.
  • Only tournament staff can watch it. It is deleted after 30 days unless it is part of a fair-play case.
  • Nothing else on your phone is read. A referee, not software, decides any outcome.
  • Declining is not a penalty. You can play on an organizer device instead.

2. Record the match

  1. Turn on Do Not Disturb, so private notifications do not appear in the video.
  2. Open Control Center and tap the Screen Recording button. If it is missing, add it in Settings, then Control Center.
  3. Play the whole match without stopping the recording.
  4. Stop the recording, come back to this page and upload the video below.

Keep this page open in Safari. If it closes, open it again on this iPhone before the match window ends and your pairing will still be here.

3. Upload the recording

Stay on this page and on the event Wi-Fi until the upload finishes. A full match can take a few minutes.
