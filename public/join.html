    Before you go to the referee

    Check these yourself. Any of them blocks clearance until it is fixed and the referee checks again.

    1. Developer Mode is offSettings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode.
    2. No Enterprise or Developer App is trustedSettings > General > VPN & Device Management. Remove any your school or work did not install.
    3. No sideloading or jailbreak appsFor example TrollStore, AltStore, Scarlet, ESign or Sileo.
    4. The game's App Store page says OpenIf it says Get, delete your copy and install it from the App Store.
    5. Only one copy of the gameDelete any second or renamed copy.
    6. No VPN or extra profileTurn off VPNs and remove DNS or configuration profiles you do not need.
    Turn on Do Not Disturb so private notifications do not pop up during the match.
