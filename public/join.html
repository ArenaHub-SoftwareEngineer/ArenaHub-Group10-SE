Before you play

This tournament asks players to agree to match monitoring. Here is exactly what that means for your device:

    It never reads your messages, photos, contacts, location, passwords or what other apps contain. Data stays on the referee laptop at the venue and is deleted after 30 days unless it is part of a fair-play case. A referee, not software, decides any outcome.

    You can decline. Declining is not a penalty. The referee is told, and will give you an organizer device or watch your match in person.

    Android: use the ArenaHub Companion app

    1. First time only: download and install the app. If the phone asks, allow your browser to install apps. Download the app (2 MB)
    2. Open the app with your pairing code filled in, then choose Agree or Decline inside the app. Open ArenaHub Companion

    The app runs in the background during the match and stops on its own when the match window closes.

    PC: run the ArenaHub agent

    1. Download the agent. Windows may say it is unsigned: choose "More info", then "Run anyway". Download the agent (90 MB)
    2. Open Command Prompt in your Downloads folder and run this, then type YES to agree:
    3. For Dota 2, restart the game after the agent starts.

    iPhone: check in

    Nothing is installed and nothing on your iPhone is checked. A referee records the match from the observer slot in the custom room. Checking in tells the referee you agreed and are ready.

    You are checked in

    Play your match normally. The referee is recording from the observer slot.

    Tip: turn on Do Not Disturb so private notifications do not pop up during the match.

    The referee asked for my own screen recording

    Only if the referee asks. Record with Control Center, then choose the video here. Stay on the event Wi-Fi until it finishes.
