Before you play

Complete anti-cheat requirements before match admission. Android and Windows players need a current companion scan. iPhone players need a referee inspection, recording and continuous supervision.

This tournament asks players to agree to match monitoring. Here is exactly what that means for your device:

    It never reads your messages, photos, contacts, location, passwords or what other apps contain. Reports and any recordings go to the tournament API server shown in this page's address and are deleted after 30 days unless staff place an evidence hold. Screen captures can include private notifications; close personal apps before playing. A referee, not software, decides any outcome.

    You can decline. Declining is not a penalty. Staff are told. Ask them for a supported device to play on and complete the required scan.

    Android: use the ArenaHub Companion app

    1. First time only: download and install the app. If the phone asks, allow your browser to install apps. Download the app (2 MB)
    2. Open the app with your pairing code filled in, then choose Agree or Decline inside the app. Open ArenaHub Companion

    The app runs in the background during the match and stops on its own when the match window closes.

    PC: run the ArenaHub agent

    1. Download the agent. Verify this download and its fingerprint with tournament staff. This test build is unsigned; use only a trusted event device until staff validate and sign the release. Download the agent (90 MB)
    2. Double-click the downloaded app. Enter the tournament server address and your match scan code, then type YES to agree. You can also run this command from its folder:
    3. Wait for Scan passed before joining. If restricted software is listed, close it and let the agent scan again. Keep the agent open. For Dota 2 live data, restart the game after the agent starts.

    iPhone anti-cheat

    Agree here, then bring your playing iPhone to the referee. They will inspect the device and game account, review the official game listing and device settings, verify recording and arrange continuous supervision.

    Safari shows your clearance status and accepts recording uploads when enabled by staff. Installed-app and game-memory inspection are unavailable.

    You are checked in

    Waiting for the referee to inspect your iPhone.

    Bring this playing device to the referee before joining. Supervised clearance lasts 15 minutes for admission. Maintain referee supervision and recording throughout play. Tell staff if you change devices or supervision stops.

    Tip: turn on Do Not Disturb so private notifications do not pop up during the match.

    What the referee will check on your iPhone

    Any of these blocks clearance until it is removed and the referee checks again:

    • Developer Mode is on (Settings > Privacy & Security).
    • An enterprise or developer app is trusted (Settings > General > VPN & Device Management).
    • A sideloading or jailbreak app is installed, for example TrollStore, AltStore, ESign or Sileo.
    • The game's App Store page shows Get instead of Open, or there are two copies of the game.
    • A VPN or profile the venue did not give you is turned on.
